Moonvane — Privacy Policy & Support

Effective: 1 January 2027 · Version: 1.1.0

Moonvane is a puzzle game. The web version stores your progress (level, stars, gems, settings) only in your browser's local storage. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data on the web version.

The iOS app may show ads via Google AdMob and offer in-app purchases via Apple. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve ads, subject to your App Tracking Transparency choice and regional consent (GDPR/UMP). Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. You can remove ads with a one-time purchase.

Third-party data practices: Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

We do not knowingly collect data from children. The game contains no user-generated content and no account system.
